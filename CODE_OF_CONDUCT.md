# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Focus feedback on the work, welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and respect privacy and intellectual property.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or block participation to protect the community. Enforcement and support are best effort, with no guaranteed response time.

To report abusive content, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant comment or user profile. Do not post sensitive details publicly. For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
